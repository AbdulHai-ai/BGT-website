Example workflow

How it fits together.

Illustrative example

  1. Brief and scriptGoals, audience, message and format.
  2. StoryboardScenes, presenter and tone agreed before generation.
  3. GenerationShots, voices and presenters produced and selected.
  4. Edit and finishEditing, grading, sound and captions.
  5. Versions deliveredAspect ratios and language versions.

Responsible use: AI presenters are presented as AI-generated and never as real customers or fabricated testimonials. Voice and likeness are used only with documented consent.

The engagement can include

  • Concept, script and storyboard
  • Presenter and voice setup, with consent
  • Master video and variations
  • Localised language versions
  • Platform-ready formats
  • Revision rounds

Delivery approach

Discover, architect, build, support.

  1. DiscoverWe agree goals, audience and the story to tell.
  2. ArchitectWe plan scenes, presenter and voice before generating.
  3. BuildWe produce, select and edit the strongest takes.
  4. SupportWe deliver the versions you need and iterate.

Questions

Are AI videos labelled as AI?

We recommend disclosure wherever platform rules or regulations require it, and we do not present AI presenters as real customers.

Can you clone a voice?

Only with the documented consent of the person whose voice or likeness is used.

Can AI be combined with real footage?

Yes. Our media and creative production team can combine filmed material with AI-generated shots.
