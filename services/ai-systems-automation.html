AI systems that handle enquiries, bookings and follow-up.

Leads go cold because follow-up depends on someone remembering.

Example workflow

How it fits together.

Illustrative example

  1. Enquiry arrivesFrom WhatsApp, Instagram, a call or your website.
  2. Agent replies and qualifiesAnswers common questions and gathers the details you need.
  3. Booking offeredAvailable slots are shown and confirmed.
  4. Reminders sentConfirmations and reminders go out automatically.
  5. Follow-upQuiet leads are followed up on your schedule.
  6. CRM updatedThe record, stage and notes are kept current.

Human handoff: when a conversation needs a person, such as a complaint, a complex request or a high-value lead, the agent passes it over with the full conversation history.

The engagement can include

  • Workflow mapping and requirements
  • Agent design: tone, rules and escalation paths
  • Channel, calendar and CRM integration
  • Testing against realistic conversations
  • Launch and monitoring
  • Ongoing improvements

  4. SupportWe monitor results and keep refining.

Questions

Can a person take over a conversation?

Yes. Handoff and escalation rules are part of the design, so staff can step in with the full history.

Which tools can you integrate with?

We integrate through the public APIs and webhooks of common messaging platforms, calendars and CRMs, and confirm compatibility during discovery. This does not imply an official partnership with any platform.

Will customers know they are speaking to AI?

We set up disclosure and handoff wording with you, in line with your policies and the rules that apply to your market.
