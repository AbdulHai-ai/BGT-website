The engagement

A clear scope.
A considered outcome.

Define the operational core first, then build the modules and integrations that support it. The agreed scope can include:

  • Process mapping, requirements, and role permissions
  • Data models, interface design, and module specifications
  • Custom application development and integrations
  • Data migration planning, testing, and user acceptance
  • Documentation, training, and agreed ongoing support

A few useful answers

Before we begin.

Can different branches have different permissions?

Yes. Roles and branch access can be designed around your operating structure. We agree who can view, create, approve, and change each type of record.

Can payroll and commissions follow our own rules?

Yes. Salary, commission, incentive, and penalty calculations can reflect agreed business rules. We document those rules, test representative cases, and include review or approval steps where required.

Can we start with a single module?

Yes. A focused module such as staff records, attendance, or a management dashboard can be a starting point. The architecture should account for the integrations and modules you may need later.

What happens to our existing data?

Discovery covers data sources, quality, and ownership. Where migration is in scope, we define the mapping, validation, and transition process before moving records.
