Example workflow

How it fits together.

Illustrative example

  1. Agree the rulesPay structures, shifts, roles and approval steps.
  2. Model the businessBranches, teams, permissions and records.
  3. Build in modulesStaff, attendance, payroll, then reporting.
  4. Connect and migrateLink existing tools and bring across current records.
  5. ReportDashboards for owners and managers.

Payroll and calculations are configured to the business rules agreed with you. We do not claim universal legal compliance; confirm statutory requirements with your accountant or adviser.

The engagement can include

  • Requirements workshop with owners and managers
  • Role, branch and data model
  • Staged module development
  • Import of existing records where suitable
  • Training and handover
  • Support and further development

Delivery approach

Discover, architect, build, support.

  1. DiscoverWe learn how each branch and role really works.
  2. ArchitectWe design the data, rules and screens before building.
  3. BuildWe release in stages you can review and test.
  4. SupportWe support your team and extend the system as needs change.

Questions

Can it replace our spreadsheets?

In most cases yes. We review the current sheets, define the rules, and import records where they are clean enough.

Is payroll compliant with labour law?

Payroll is configured to the rules you agree with us. Statutory requirements should be confirmed with your accountant or adviser.

Is it web or mobile?

Systems are typically web-based and usable on phones and desktops. Native apps are scoped separately if needed.
